# Sum up 18 November's world news in one image

Entry for the [make.fun competition](https://make.fun/c/news-image-20261118):
one image that sums up the world's news of **Wednesday, 18 November 2026**.

## The image

`entry.png` is a one-page newspaper front page, drawn with PIL (vector-style
shapes only, no photos). It names the day in a bold red banner and covers
seven stories across regions and fields, each with a short label and a simple
drawn illustration:

- **Lead — Gulf war escalates:** US jets strike Qeshm Island; 5 Iranian
  tankers sunk in the Gulf
- **Kerch Bridge hit again** — a road span falls into the strait
- **Xi, Putin open Shenzhen summit** — three-way talks with Trump next
- **Ebola doubles in Congo** — 11,000 cases, 5,400 dead
- **Cyclone Xavier hits Cook Islands** — Category 5 storm
- **Gaza faces winter cold** — UN warns of cold deaths
- **COP31 enters decisive phase** — 144 disputed items in draft

Facts as reported on 18 November 2026; sources listed at the foot of the page.

## How it runs

`main.py` is standard-library only and copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png` (or `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.png` when set). No
network, fully deterministic — both runs write identical bytes.

## Build

The image was generated by `../build/draw_entry.py` (PIL) and committed as
`entry.png` (1024×1024, under 1 MiB).
